Biography of Dhanuja Wanniarachchi

Dhanuja Wanniarachchi is a Research Scientist at Singapore Management University SMU, School of Computing and Information Systems, Singapore. His research focuses on practical edge intelligence systems, multimodal perception, collaborative sensing, computer vision, edge AI, and intelligent systems for real-world deployments.

He completed his Bachelor of Science degree in Computing and has actively contributed to research in collaborative edge perception, multimodal sensing, and efficient AI systems. His research works include SteerCam, ComAI, and FusionBridge, focusing on lightweight collaboration, multimodal perception, and efficient inference for edge environments.

Dhanuja has presented his research at leading international conferences including ACM SenSys and IEEE INFOCOM. His work explores efficient multimodal perception, collaborative inference, sensor fusion, and edge intelligence applications.

Alongside his research career, Dhanuja has five years of experience as a television presenter at Sri Lanka Rupavahini Corporation. His presenting experience includes live programme presenting, children's news presenting, and children's day live programmes.

His technical expertise includes Artificial Intelligence, Deep Learning, Computer Vision, Edge Computing, Multimodal Learning, Python, Flutter, React, C#, JavaScript, PHP, MySQL, SQL, IoT systems, and software development.
