Introduction

Dhanuja Wanniarachchi is a recent Ph.D. graduate in Computer Science from Singapore Management University, advised by Prof. Archan Misra. His research focuses on collaborative AI, multimodal sensing and sense-making, mobile and distributed systems, deep learning, computer vision, edge intelligence, and reinforcement learning-based real-world applications.

His work explores how intelligent systems can operate efficiently in real-world edge environments by combining multiple sensing modalities, distributed intelligence, and lightweight collaborative inference.
